No matter your age, position, or standing in any sense of the real world, Halloween is a time for everyone to get involved. This extends to the seniors, the generation that built the America we know today. As the grateful youth, Our Beautiful Barnegat hosted a trick or treat hour of fun for the inhabitants of our town's eldercare facility, exercising our appreciation and care from handing out candy, writing and delivering cards of kind words, and to dressing up as ghouls and ghosts to embrace the season's tradition.
