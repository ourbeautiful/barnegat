The beautiful and well-seasoned East Bay section of Barnegat is now on film! As Connor Meehan and AJ Smith recorded the sights of Barnegat's historic and culinary side, many more on social media can interact and learn about the area. This can help both an interest in the town and the small businesses that make it.
