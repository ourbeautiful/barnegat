As the spooky season brews, and Halloween Day comes closer and closer, it is OBB's greatest honor to raise the spirits of all citizens alike, whether you are 5 years old, or on your way to the office. We have decorated our local bus stops to match that promise; to match that once engulfing feeling of the Halloween tradition. No matter your age, holidays are important. They are important for our culture and way of life, allowing all to enjoy the silly, but scary, fun as a united community.
