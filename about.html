About Us

Civic pride. Love of nature. Young leadership.

Our Beautiful Barnegat is the founding chapter of Our Beautiful America — a new kind of civic organization, built by someone who refused to wait for a seat at the table.

The Spark Behind OBA

Built for people who were told to wait

I grew up loving my community, wanting a hand in shaping it. But when I looked for a seat at the table, my age was the reason I was told to wait.

So instead of waiting, I decided to build a table of my own.

Inspired by visionaries

Henry Ford, Walt Disney, and Howard Hughes — American builders who turned bold ideas into lasting institutions. Big dreams just need someone willing to start.

Rooted in Barnegat

Where the Pinelands meet the Bay

Barnegat sits where two treasures meet — the quiet, tea-colored streams of the Pinelands, and the open water of Barnegat Bay.

Sandy pine forests, salt marsh, and shoreline, all within reach of home. That's what Our Beautiful Barnegat is here to protect, restore, and share with the next generation.
